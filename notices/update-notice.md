### JET Pilot 2.0

Every cluster, every cloud, one calm app: a new Clusters hub, AWS, Google Cloud, Azure and six more clouds built in, sign-in without leaving the app, production guardrails and rebuilt settings. Your settings move over by themselves, and your own kubeconfig is never changed.

### Updating from 1.38.1 or older

After clicking **Update now**, give it a minute before you click **Restart**: older versions offer the restart before the update has finished downloading. Still on the old version after restarting? Download the latest version from [jet-pilot.app](https://www.jet-pilot.app), or on macOS run `brew upgrade --cask --greedy unxsist/tap/jet-pilot`.
